# Known Issues

Project Silverfish Co-op is a proof of concept under active development. This list summarizes reports from release-candidate playtests. Check the notes for the specific ZIP you installed. Fixes verified in development can still need a live two-PC retest. [Release notes](https://github.com/hypnotoadtrance/silverfish-coop/releases)

## Current priorities

1. Improve host and guest frame pacing, local interaction response, and network latency. Compare Observatory and Crossroads under load, track packet volume and queue delays, and work toward a 100 ms round trip where the connection allows it.
2. Rework co-op menus and flows, starting with hosting, joining, and Steam invites. Keep stock menus for gameplay where the game already provides them.
3. Return to visual and animation polish after the performance and UI/UX passes. Gameplay regressions stay in the functional backlog.

The `rc-f0b1c06` Crossroads baseline used two muted Direct IP instances. During the short guest-walking sample, the longest observed intervals were 24 ms for the host and 23 ms for the guest. Status publication took 11 ms and 16 ms inside those intervals. The guest measured about 2 ms of loopback keepalive RTT and reported no expired or evicted relay frames. These samples guide the next game-thread and network measurements. Weather, critters, the Lingering Teleport, placed loot, and mushrooms passed. The shared cash notification check timed out and remains open.

`rc-8b1221c` starts the targeted Lua-to-C++ migration with a single native read for health, regeneration, hunger, stim, radiation, fatigue, intoxication, and sickness on each bound guest. On both seats in Observatory and Crossroads, 1,024 reads took 1 to 2 ms through the C++ reader and 8 to 10 ms through the former Lua reader. All eight values matched, and mismatched pawn and save addresses were refused. The same two muted Direct IP launches passed food and medicine, a cold stock crate, Crossroads travel and weather, critters, the Lingering Teleport, and mushrooms. The original saves were preserved. The largest Crossroads guest walking interval was still 59 ms, with 20 ms spent inside canister status gathering. Whole-frame performance and Steam latency remain open.

`rc-be31b46` moves placed canister status reads into C++. In isolated Crossroads runs, the largest canister status samples were 1 ms and 5 ms, compared with 20 ms in the preceding build. Across host and guest, 1,024 native reads took 1 to 3 ms and matched the Lua reads, which took 13 to 21 ms. Both seats saw stock propane movement and a fuel drum's ignition and destruction. The guest applied the ignition and received one explosion cue. The muted test did not observe an active continuous flame or report creation of blast particles. Those effects need a rendered two-PC retest. The original saves were preserved. Crossroads still had a guest walking interval of 58 ms, and Steam latency remains open.

`rc-3ae5434` batches stock jammer transforms in C++. Each 1,024-read comparison took 4 to 6 ms natively and 40 to 110 ms in Lua. All nine pose values matched for both seats in Crossroads and Black Lake, and mismatched world addresses were rejected. On the same two muted Direct IP launches, the party accepted and turned in both Simmi quests with matching journals and shared payouts. Crossroads weather travel, critters, mushrooms, and stock canister state and cue dispatch also passed. The largest jammer status sample was 1 ms. The largest overall Crossroads intervals were still 67 ms on host and 55 ms on guest, so frame pacing remains open. The test preserved the original saves.

`rc-47b0a75` releases the in-process bridge lock before copying status and command replies. Pose and footstep readers can retry on their next update when the bridge is busy. A standalone C++ test held the lock while checking that behavior, retained queued poses, and verified that concurrently copied status data matched its revision. The first two muted launches passed lean projection and release, footstep audio in both directions, prop carry and impacts, and Crossroads weather travel. The guest's stock lean key input was inconclusive. Food initially reached a guest marked downed or respawning after the impact fixture. A second pair passed both checks using separate campaigns in the same processes. Both runs preserved the original saves. The longest Crossroads intervals were 79 ms on host and 59 ms on guest, so overall frame pacing remains open.

`rc-18e85a7` uses a current-world Unreal query for stock merchant discovery. The host's sampled lookups peaked at 0.11 ms in Observatory and 0.03 ms in Crossroads. The same two muted launches verified discovery of the ShopKeep merchant, the two-stack eye contract, roaming barter, guest crate opening, and Crossroads weather travel. The original saves were preserved. The profiler now records elapsed milliseconds and game-thread CPU cycles separately. During one 69 ms Crossroads host interval, status publication took 54 ms and used 8.8 times its same-window median CPU cycles. The guest's longest Crossroads interval was 70 ms, with world inspection and player synchronization among the largest timed calls. Frame pacing remains open.

`rc-0cae3d6` formats status rows in C++. On both seats in Observatory and Crossroads, 4,096 representative rows matched the Lua output byte for byte and took 4.2 to 6.3 ms in C++, compared with 13.9 to 18.6 ms in Lua. The grouped run passed roaming barter, Castaway dialogue, guest crate opening, and weather travel. The Simmi check needed two focused reruns after a missed transition fixture acknowledgement and a Black Lake firing-position search failure. The final run verified both objectives and turn-ins on the same ZIP. All six isolated launches preserved the original saves. Crossroads still had a 74 ms idle host interval, so frame pacing remains open.

`rc-b0fe29f` checks the active stock menu before qualifying the Journal's host and viewport, and reuses formatted canister rows when their live state is unchanged. The grouped run passed guest crate opening, critters, mushrooms, propane movement, and fuel drum ignition, destruction, and blast cue dispatch. The muted test still reported no continuous flame or blast particle creation. The stock Journal opened and closed on a focused follow-up pair after correcting the fixture entry point. No closed-menu sample reached the full Journal context check. The largest sampled Journal update was 0.008 ms, and canister rows peaked at 0.112 ms. Both pairs preserved the original saves. Crossroads still reached a 69 ms host interval, and live Steam invite verification remains open.
