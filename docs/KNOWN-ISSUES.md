# Known Issues

Project Silverfish Co-op is a proof of concept under active development. This list summarizes reports from release-candidate playtests. Check the notes for the specific ZIP you installed. Fixes verified in development can still need a live two-PC retest. [Release notes](https://github.com/hypnotoadtrance/silverfish-coop/releases)

## Being worked on

| Area | Known issue |
| --- | --- |
| Enemy and allied NPC combat (current priority) | In a previous two-PC warehouse fight, the guest's shots appeared to hit robots without killing them. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-da1a69c`, a full-health Tutorial Dock worker took damage from the guest’s Rusty Pistol, retaliated against that guest, and died with the same ragdoll on both seats. The trial staged retreats between shots. An earlier autonomous State Conscript and Bandit fight produced matching death and ragdoll states. A later muted pair verified a stock Heavy Pipe swing from the guest against the original warehouse worker. Its host health fell from 120 to 57 and the host credited the guest. Sustained two-PC fights and other NPC encounters remain open. |
| Guest weapon inputs and presentation | In playtesting, the host missed the guest's 9mm Revolver shot, manual reload could fail, and the guest saw an offset muzzle flash or a missing reload animation. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-82b9542`, the guest reloaded and fired the 9mm Revolver, Obrez Cut-Down Rifle with incendiary ammo, suppressed Heavy Pistol, .50-cal semi-auto, and scoped Type 33. In `rc-63d8249`, the Rusty Pistol attached one flash to the guest's live muzzle, and the stock first-person reload animation started and advanced. Foreground aiming, alternate ammunition, and special firing mechanisms still need coverage. |
| Party wipe and respawn | The stock keep-item screen waits for both confirmations. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-ac8877d`, the screen opened over the host’s Pause and the guest’s Journal. The guest’s confirmed choice stayed visible and read-only, including after reopening. Both returned to play after the host confirmed, and the guest retained only the selected item. In a foreground test, confirm in both orders and check weapon visibility after respawn. Keep whole stacks when selecting items. |
| Quests and bounty boards | Some eligible offers have disappeared from Simmi's jobs and the physical board. In a muted Direct IP session on `rc-87c577c`, the guest accepted Crossroads and the host accepted Black Lake. Both peers saw the same jammer positions. The guest destroyed both jammers, then the host turned in Crossroads and the guest turned in Black Lake through Simmi's stock menu. Both journals updated, the shared balance rose by $200 and then $400, and each player earned 2 XP across the two jobs. Repeat turn-in requests left the balance unchanged. A ShopKeep test also redeemed one guest Eye while leaving the host’s three-Eye stack and the guest’s second stack intact. The shared balance increased by $801, and the receipt credited each player 1 XP. Other contracts, randomized placements across additional campaign seeds, and foreground sessions still need coverage. |
| Crossroads weather | After traveling, a guest could see rain while the host had clear skies. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-ff5b742`, the guest applied the host's clear, wet, and clear states after entering Crossroads, silenced an independently active stock rain actor, and received the stock siren once. Retest visible rain and audible warnings in a foreground session. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-436d484`, one stock lightning strike played the bolt, ground burst, flash, and thunder at the host’s impact point on the guest. The test verified effect dispatch and flash registration. Check the visible effects and audible thunder in foreground play. On `rc-66edf28`, a muted Direct IP test joined and reconnected during a running lightning storm. Rain state now comes directly from the host's stock controller. The guest dispatched the storm's wind and ambient particles once, blocked a conflicting local storm, applied the host's Pestilence fog parameters, and cleared its fog when the host did. A warning the busy host horn ignored did not replay for the guest. Retest visible and audible storm effects in a foreground two-PC session. |

## Queued and awaiting wider verification

| Area | Known issue |
| --- | --- |
| Guest worn gear | Guests reported being unable to remove or drop equipped gear. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-87c577c`, a guest dropped a helmet through the stock **Drop** menu and dragged chest armor into **Nearby Items**. Both peers saw each drop, and the host picked up the exact items using stock Interact. Returning gear to the guest backpack also passed. Retest the two slots in a foreground session. |
| Guest medical items | Guests reported that some medicine could not be used. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-b4c2627`, the guest used Dirty Bandage, Gauze, Medical Salve, Large Medkit, Stim Pills, and Antibiotics through the base-game inventory. The test verified health and regeneration on the guest, sickness and antibiotic resistance on the host's copy of the guest character, and consumption of one unit from a two-item Gauze stack. The host character and campaign checkpoint stayed intact. Foreground and Steam verification remain open. |
| Guest weapon unloading | A guest reported that **Take Ammo** was missing from the context menu for a loaded gun. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-82b9542`, eight cases across six guns used the stock menu from the backpack, an inactive quick slot, or the active slot. The weapon stayed in place and the round returned to the guest's backpack. Foreground and Steam verification remain open, particularly for alternate loads and larger magazines. |
| Downed controls | In published builds, a player downed while aiming can stay zoomed in. The development fix calls the game's aim-cancel routine. An isolated two-player test verified that the guest stopped aiming and crouched when downed. |
| Held items and animations | The host previously missed the guest's melee swing, and a melee weapon appeared to float near the remote character's feet. `rc-58c6a8d` uses the stock weapon rig's grip point. A muted Direct IP test on `rc-da1a69c` verified an Old Pipe tap, a charged hold, and a buffered combo through the original input. Wider combat tests remain open. In one check the test swings missed a staged NPC, while charged Heavy Pipe strikes broke the stock office board. Retest hand alignment and swings with more equipped models and on an internet connection. |
| Roaming NPC barter | The playtest reported failed exchanges and stale offers on ordinary talkable NPCs. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-fb57442`, a dynamically spawned stock freelancer generated different trader IDs on each seat. The guest's stock screen stayed hidden until it loaded the host's inventory. The guest bought one bandage, both views lost that item, and the shared wallet changed once. Retest with naturally encountered roaming NPCs and with the guest's menu open during a host purchase. |
| Warehouse switches | In `rc-8834cf0`, a muted Direct IP pair used the placed Tutorial Dock circuit box. The host switched it off and the guest used stock Interact to switch it on. Both peers showed the same lever, indicator, and seven linked lights. Playtest the switch again in the foreground, including when joining after it has been toggled. |
| Warehouse boards | In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-da1a69c`, a guest’s normal Heavy Pipe swing left the office board intact at its stock damage threshold. Two stock charged swings broke it on the host and cleared its mesh and collision on the guest. Guest-local damage alone left the shared board intact. Retest other weapons and tools in a foreground session, including after joining an existing campaign. |
| Breakable physics props | A previous playtest reported mismatched bottle destruction and missing glass effects. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-fce9f11`, a host impact and a guest throw each broke a stock Tutorial Dock bottle on both peers. The guest received one break cue at each impact. Check the glass sound and visible splash in a foreground session, and try other breakable props. |
| Flashlights | Both seats heard the switch but missed the other player's beam. The held beam could point more than 60 degrees away from the owner's light. `rc-38b406e` makes it follow the player's replicated aim. The muted Direct IP check covers both directions and the stock C hold to raise and lower the offhand torch. Check the light on walls and floors in a foreground session, including the heavy flashlight. |
| Guest consumables | The guest could use an MRE but reported being unable to eat Leftovers and Clyde’s Honey Egg. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-b4c2627`, all three worked through the base-game inventory and restored the guest's hunger meter. A guest eating a mushroom previously appeared to use it at hip level. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-fd3d5ca`, the guest ate a Brown Mushroom through the stock inventory and the host saw its mesh move to 24 cm in front of the avatar's animated head. Check hand alignment and the eating sound on both PCs. Consumables with special effects still need broader coverage. |
| Mod-added consumables | `rc-d050b29` can apply food, health, regeneration, and stimulant changes from items that inherit the stock consumable effects without requiring individual recipes. An isolated two-player test used Live Weta through stock inventory click and drag, verified one item consumed per use, and recovered the guest’s state after reconnecting. Third-party items need playtesting. Custom scripted effects, quest-item confirmations, timed effects, infection changes, and shared-world effects still need explicit support. |
| Thrown bolts | A guest sometimes missed short flights and had no interactable bolt at the landing point. `rc-aab52b4` added a short-flight visual and `rc-b69a66f` shares the stock ground bolt through the same system as other world loot. In a muted Direct IP pair, each player used Interact to collect the other player's bolt. Each pickup added one bolt, removed it on both seats, and left the campaign checkpoint unchanged. Check flight visibility, impact audio, and pickups in a foreground session. |
| Thrown pipe bombs | A guest previously missed the host's throw and detonation. A muted Direct IP test on `rc-a1c603d` covered throws from both seats. The guest cooked a pipe bomb before release, the host used its remaining fuse, and the guest's stock quick slot lost one bomb. Both peers saw each projectile in flight and clear after one stock detonation. Check the visible blast, nearby sound, and throws after a longer cooking hold in a foreground session. |
| Live critters | A guest previously saw a frog move without its hop and could not catch it. In a muted Direct IP pair on `rc-a26d7f1`, a stock Crossroads frog switched to its hop mesh on the guest, and the guest caught it with Interact. Both peers lost the world actor and only the guest received a Live Frog. Wetas and beetles use the same pickup path and still need wider playtesting. |
| Placed loot on revisits | A previously collected Intro bandage reappeared for the guest while the host still saw it as taken. In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-86e2774`, the host's stock copy was absent and the guest's copy stayed hidden after returning to Tutorial Dock. Retest longer loot replenishment cycles in a live campaign. |
| Shovel digging | In a muted Direct IP test on `rc-da1a69c`, both players dug ordinary holes. The guest excavated and collected the physical Missing Contact package, kept the exact item after reconnecting, and redeemed it through the stock Black Market bounty board. The party received $450 and both players earned 1 XP. Foreground Interact and carrying unearthed props still need coverage. |
| Ragdoll carrying | Guest-carried bodies moved smoothly in the two-PC report. The guest saw host-carried bodies snap toward the pickup point. `rc-407dc7b` updates the host grip and interpolates the guest view. The muted Direct IP check follows a stock humanoid during the carry, checks where both bodies settle, and confirms the guest can target the released body. Retest the warehouse robots, stairs, and Steam play. |
| Enemies and anomalies | Development tests have verified a flying drone’s death and ragdoll and several host-owned stock traps. The Crossroads Travel spawner now shares Lingering Teleport movement and guest arrival, with a spatial cue at each endpoint. Foreground particles, other spawn populations, and additional encounters remain open. |
| Travel and presentation | The host once stopped while the guest was returning after Observatory-to-Crossroads travel. `rc-da1a69c` waits for the destination’s listener and roster to be ready before reopening guest admission. The grouped Direct IP run crossed Observatory, Crossroads, Black Lake, and the Black Market with the same guest. Foreground checks remain for the 15-meter regroup rule, transition audio, the fade, and the guest’s first visible frame on arrival. |
| Guest equipment after travel | Guests could still see a gun in hand after changing maps, but the host could lose its selection and stop processing fire input. `rc-5649dbc` reads the held stock item after travel. An isolated Direct IP guest fired the same Light Pistol in Black Lake without reselecting it, carried the updated magazine back to Observatory, and rejoined after a link drop. Retest other weapons, tools, and Steam sessions. |
| Performance and connection recovery | Players have reported interaction delay, Crossroads frame hitches, and visible corrections while moving props. `rc-ac8877d` spreads loose-item qualification across updates. In a muted Direct IP test, a guest carried and dropped a stock Crossroads physics item, then picked it up once using Interact. Both views agreed on the resting position and the pickup. Stash scans and remaining frame hitches still need profiling. Steam reconnect, lobby discovery, and behavior under internet latency need wider testing. |
| Menu and code cleanup | Stock UI integration, hosting and join usability, and removal of obsolete development paths remain on the backlog. |

Recent playtests confirmed individual death-backpack recovery, food restoring the guest's hunger, and current and lasting damage applying to the guest.

When reporting a bug, include the release tag, map or landmark, exact item or NPC if known, whether you used Steam or Direct IP, and what the host and guest each saw. [Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/hypnotoadtrance/silverfish-coop/issues/new)

For detailed engineering checks, see the [integration notes](IN-GAME-INTEGRATION.md) and [native test coverage](NATIVE-INTEGRATION.md).
